# Free single-image building-to-3D options

**Checked 4 Nov 2026.** For replicating real buildings, none of the reviewed single-image AI services should be treated as a measurement-grade reconstruction. They infer plausible hidden geometry; they do not recover it from the one view. Best recommendation for production fidelity: trace/model the buildings as clean low-poly geometry against the saved reference crops, using dimensions or additional views where available. Use a free AI generator only as a disposable shape experiment, not as the source of truth. No image was uploaded, and no account was created.

## Recommendation and interpretation

1. **Production:** use traced low-poly modeling from the supplied building crops, manually controlling footprint, height, roofline, openings and facade details. Keep the image as a reference texture only if appropriate; do not treat generated details as evidence.
2. **Optional free experiment:** Meshy is the clearest verified no-payment download test if an untextured Meshy 6 Lite asset is acceptable; the export cap/model restrictions are explicit. Tripo is a second test if a public, non-commercial free output is acceptable; its pricing page states 15 H2.5 exports, but check current availability and file format in-app before relying on it. No signup or generation was performed here.
3. **Why these are not photogrammetry:** a single aerial/satellite view contains no observed side/rear surfaces or independent depth observations. Image-to-3D systems infer occluded surfaces from learned priors, so unseen walls, roofs, height and window placement may be invented or distorted. The SPAR3D paper explicitly frames single-image reconstruction as ill-posed and describes occluded-region uncertainty; specialist building-facade research instead uses image parsing/geometry methods and discusses perspective distortion. Photogrammetry is a different method requiring multiple overlapping views; Polycam’s official workflow recommends substantial frame overlap and multiple capture passes for space capture. [SPAR3D paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.04689) · [building facade study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.04406) · [Polycam photogrammetry workflow](https://poly.cam/tools/photogrammetry)

## Could not confirm

- Any hosted Tencent Hunyuan3D free-credit/export policy, required account, precise web export formats, or hosted image-retention terms.
- A Meshy Free-tier guarantee that the Lite download offers GLB/OBJ specifically (general Meshy format marketing is not plan/model-specific).
- Whether Tripo retains uploaded images for a particular duration; the surfaced privacy policy describes user-content processing and retention but the retention schedule was not established in this review.
- That any single-image generator can reproduce exact dimensions, unseen building faces, or architectural details faithfully. These tools are generative, not a substitute for multi-view measurement or reconstruction.

**Confidence:** high for Meshy’s current free download restrictions and Tripo’s stated free-plan limits/formats; moderate for privacy interpretation and differences among product versions; low for hosted Tencent free access/export because official current details were not verified. Pricing and plan limits are moving targets; re-check before testing.
