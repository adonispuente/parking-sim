# Five-bus shuttle route review (read-only)

**Verdict.** The trace identifies home, two B pickups, and three C pickups, but it does **not** establish a complete, directed A-home → B/C → A-home bus loop. In particular, the currently modeled A public exit feeds the northbound-only western feeder **above** Corporate Drive; a bus cannot lawfully turn back south on that feeder to reach B/C using the existing directed graph. Do not call an on-screen line physically verified merely because endpoints can be joined. ## Route recommendation, conditional on surveying internal aisles

1. **Home departure:** pickup → a *verified Lot A internal drive* → `Lot A Main exit` → western feeder northbound. **STOP:** this outlet is around y≈20–35 and Corporate Drive meets the feeder around y≈260–302. The feeder rule disallows increasing y at x<48; the public A exit therefore has no demonstrated forward route to Corporate/B/C. A different legal A outbound connector, legal southbound street route, or authorized turnaround must be traced before implementing a complete loop. Do not send unbadged buses via `Lot A Black Permit Entrance` just to bypass this gap.
2. **If a lawful A→Corporate connection is confirmed:** Corporate Drive eastbound reaches `Lot B entrance` near (268–300, 200–223) for **B south**, then a *provisional internal B aisle* would be needed to the actual pickup (453.734,215.059). For **B north**, Corporate reaches the north `Lot B exit` near x440–473/y39–68; the car router currently reverses that exit throat for inbound B-north access. Confirm bus inbound permission and an internal aisle from that throat to (386.602,94.466). These are **two B stops**, so assigning four B buses requires an explicit distribution rule; none is established by the trace.
3. **C approaches:** Corporate eastbound reaches the staffed `Lot C  entrance` near x480–553/y43–73. From that entrance, separately trace internal aisle/pullout connections to C roundabout (496.191,125.968), C north (633.796,123.498), and C south (524.128,227.119); the satellite shows lot surfaces but not a labeled directed bus graph, and C south is beyond the building edge. Do not connect these three points by straight lines through buildings, parking, or the blocked B/C strip. The car router's alternate C-east arrival reverses `Lot C  exit`; validate whether buses may do that before using it.
4. **Return from B/C:** use a surveyed stop→exit aisle. B west can leave by `Lot B exit toward feeder` into Corporate and then feeder **north**, whereas B north leaves `Lot B exit` to Corporate east/Equity; C west leaves `Lot C exit 1` and C east leaves `Lot C  exit` toward Equity. For A return, prefer a validated Equity-drive circuit south and west to its labeled western `Equity Entrance`/feeder junction, then feeder north to `Lot A Main Entrance`, followed by an internal A aisle back to home. Confirm direction/turn permissions at Corporate↔Equity and Equity↔western feeder, and bus access through A main entrance; the current router validates street-polygon containment, not these turn permissions or bus clearance. B west's direct feeder-north exit has the same above-Corporate dead-end problem for reaching A; it needs a distinct verified return, not an implied U-turn.

**Evidence boundary.** `pavementRouting.js` grids only `Feeder Road`, `Corporate Drive`, `Equity Drive`, and the two Equity entrance polygons; `streetPath()` snaps a point to the nearest gridded street node and only enforces feeder northbound when x<48. It does not route through A/B/C parking aisles, enforce all road directions, provide intersection priority, or guarantee bus-body/swept-turn clearance. `vectorRoutes.js` openly calls its arrival→departure parking maneuver untraced; its A/B/C car access endpoints are not shuttle-stop coordinates. [`research/vector-routing-current.md`](vector-routing-current.md) reports street containment probes, but explicitly does **not** verify full vehicle-body containment, merge priority, or lane separation. ## Operational policy boundary

Five persistent buses share ordinary road flow: while B is below its **confirmed full capacity of 487**, four serve B (distribution between B north/south to be configured) and one serves C; when B reaches 487, all five serve C, round-robin across its three labeled stops. Make the B-full trigger adjustable without inventing a numeric definition of “mostly empty”; finish the current trip and return home before reassignment. `traffic.js` currently models randomly generated arriving vehicles with a `shuttle` flag, not five recurring stop-to-home shuttles; its spacing is per route lane, not shared-intersection collision control. Badge parking availability is a separate, unquantified operating pool, not additional general capacity: keep total 1,584 = A 411 + B 487 + C 686.
